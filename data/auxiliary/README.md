# Auxiliary data

This folder documents the recovery of candidate ages that were missing in the
raw TSJE data.

Eight titular council candidates were recorded with age = 0. Their dates of
birth were obtained through a formal request to the TSJE under Law No. 5282/2014
(Appendix B). The completed age of each candidate on the election date was
computed from those birth dates.

To protect personal data, the underlying file (containing national ID numbers,
names, surnames, and dates of birth) is not published. Instead:

- The recovered ages are recorded directly in `scripts/02_clean_data.R`.
- `TSJE_response_letter_redacted.pdf` is the official TSJE response letter documenting the source of the information.
